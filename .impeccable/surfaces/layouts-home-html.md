# Surface: personal site (home, /resume/, /blog/)

Mode: Persuade (home), Read (resume, blog). Audience: international recruiters and hiring managers; action: email or LinkedIn message. Proof: resume facts only. Constraint: resume page prints to clean PDF; no phone number.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The career as a railway timetable. Companies are stations on one line, dates are departures, the current role is the train on the platform now. Refuses the default dev-portfolio hero with avatar, bio blob, and skill pills.

OWN-WORLD: White timetable paper, black ink, one signal red (#d8231f class) used only for "now" and the primary action. Tabular figures everywhere a date appears. Ruled rows and station dots on a vertical line. A neo-grotesk workhorse face; no serif, no mono display.

STORY: In one screen the visitor reads who (name), what (Senior Backend Engineer II, Tiket.com flight booking, 100,000+ bookings a day), where (Jakarta, UTC+7, open to relocation and remote), and how to reach out. Scrolling, they follow the route back through SiCepat, Warung Pintar, Angsur, each stop with its proof. The resume page is the full timetable, printable.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A departure-board entry under a full-width ink rule: years at Tiket.com in tabular figures at left; the name at display scale with "Senior Backend Engineer II, Tiket.com" beneath; Jakarta and UTC+7; status "Open to roles" in red. No label row above the entry. Primary action "Email me" as a red square-cornered button, LinkedIn, GitHub, and Full resume beside it. Summary follows, then the route line.

FORM: Railway timetable (Kursbuch), candidate 1 of my ordered list, chosen as IMPECCABLE'S PICK; seed key 7a6cfb42. Signature interaction: the "now" dot pulses once and the route line draws downward as the stops enter view; reduced motion shows it static.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
