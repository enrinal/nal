# Surface: personal site (home, /resume/, /blog/)

Mode: Persuade (home), Read (resume, blog). Audience: international recruiters and hiring managers; action: email or LinkedIn message. Proof: resume facts only. Constraints: resume page prints to clean PDF; no phone number; no photo.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal letter, not a poster. One calm reading column where the visitor meets a person first: who I am, what I'm building now, where I've been, how to reach me. Refuses the dev-portfolio hero, skill pills, cards, and the previous timetable gimmick.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white ground with a cool tint, soft ink, one deep green accent for links and the single primary action, with a dark mode that mirrors it. Hanken Grotesk only; size and weight steps, no condensed display. Hairline rules between sections, generous spacing, no boxes.

STORY: The visitor reads a short first-person intro, sees I'm open to roles, learns what I own at Tiket.com today, skims earlier roles one line each, sees recent writing, and emails me or opens the full resume.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left-aligned 40rem column. Small header: name left, Resume and Blog right. Then a 2.4rem greeting heading, two intro paragraphs at 1.2rem, a quiet green dot line "Open to senior backend roles, remote or relocation", and the action row: "Email me" (filled accent) plus LinkedIn, GitHub, Resume as text links.

FORM: Category standard, user-pinned (calm and personal, imantumorang.com reference); no roll. Signature interaction: none beyond link underline transitions; motion stays out of the way.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
